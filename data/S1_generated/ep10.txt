EPISODE 10 - "The Test"

[00:00] INT. ASHFORD MANSION, FOYER - NIGHT
CLOSE ON Leo Bell, 6, gripping Nora's hand. His eyes lock on Julian's. Same dark eyes. Same crooked eyebrow.
LEO
Mom... why does he look like me?
Silence. Officer Reyes freezes mid-step.

[00:10] NORA BELL (29, hotel housekeeper, tired but proud) kneels, voice shaking.
NORA
Baby, go sit with Officer Reyes for a minute. Okay?
Reyes nods gently and leads Leo to the sofa.

[00:20] JULIAN ASHFORD (32, hotel owner, tailored suit, unraveling) steps toward her.
JULIAN
Seven years, Nora. I woke up in a Zurich clinic with a fake chart and a story that you'd left me. Who told me that?
NORA
(cold)
Your mother sent me a letter in your handwriting. It said you'd chosen your inheritance over me.

[00:35] All eyes turn to CELESTE ASHFORD (61, silver hair, pearls). Her smile cracks.
CELESTE
She's lying, Julian. She wants your money.
SIENNA VALE (30, fiancee, red dress) steps back, pale, twisting her $80,000 ring.
SIENNA
Celeste... you told me she was a gold digger.

[00:48] MARCUS (45, hotel manager) slips toward the door. Julian blocks it.
JULIAN
You made the call, Marcus. Why?
MARCUS
(sweating)
Because I was paid to keep her quiet. Not to ruin you.

[00:55] Dr. Hale, Julian's private physician, enters with a swab kit. Julian opens his mouth first, then looks at Leo.
JULIAN
Results in two hours.
Celeste quietly lifts her phone and texts: "Do it now."

[01:00] SMASH CUT: Headlights flood the window. A black van idles outside. A man in a gray cap steps out, holding a photo of Leo.
