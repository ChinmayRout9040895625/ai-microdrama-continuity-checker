EPISODE 1 - "The Man in the Penthouse"

[00:00] INT. GRANDVIEW HOTEL, BOSTON - SERVICE CORRIDOR - 6:12 AM
CLOSE ON a gold nameplate being screwed to a wall: "ASHFORD HOTELS." NORA BELL (29, navy housekeeper uniform, tired eyes) freezes, her cart rattling to a stop.

NORA (whispering)
Ashford...

[00:10] MARCUS (45, hotel manager, sweating through his collar) rushes up, clutching a tablet.

MARCUS
Nora, you're on the penthouse today. New owner arrives at seven. He wants it spotless. Nobody speaks to him. Understand?

NORA
Who is he?

MARCUS
Julian Ashford. Thirty-three. Bought this place for $240 million last week.

[00:22] Nora's hand trembles on the cart. FLASHBACK FLICKER: a white wedding dress, an empty church, rain on the steps. June 14, 2018.

[00:32] INT. PENTHOUSE SUITE - 7:03 AM
Nora wipes a mirror, hands shaking. Behind her, the elevator DINGS. She sees his reflection first. JULIAN ASHFORD (33, tailored charcoal suit, cold stare) stops mid-step.

JULIAN
...Nora?

She doesn't turn. A tear hits the marble counter.

NORA
You don't get to say my name.

[00:45] Her phone BUZZES. Screen: "Mrs. Alvarez, Babysitter." Photo of LEO BELL (5, gap-toothed grin, dark curls, Julian's exact green eyes). She silences it, but Julian sees the lock screen.

JULIAN (barely audible)
Who is that boy?

[00:52] The elevator opens again. CELESTE ASHFORD (60, pearls, ice-blue eyes) and SIENNA VALE (30, red dress, diamond ring) step out.

SIENNA
Darling, why is the help in your suite?

CELESTE (staring at Nora, smiling thinly)
Hello again, dear. I thought I paid you enough to stay gone.

Nora's eyes widen. Julian turns slowly toward his mother.

JULIAN
Mother... what did you pay her?

SMASH CUT TO BLACK.

[01:00] END OF EPISODE 1
