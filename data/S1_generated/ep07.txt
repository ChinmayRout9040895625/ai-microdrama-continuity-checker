EPISODE 7 - "The Bracelet"

[00:00] INT. ASHFORD ESTATE, FRONT HALL - NIGHT
A grandfather clock reads 9:14 PM. NORA BELL (32, housekeeper, still in her Grandview uniform) stares at the photo in CELESTE ASHFORD's hand: JULIAN (then 27) in a hospital bracelet, seated beside Nora's late father, FRANK BELL.

NORA
That's my dad. St. Luke's, 2018. He died that November. Why is Julian in that bed?

[00:12] Celeste (61, pearls, ice-calm) slides the photo into her pocket.

CELESTE
Ask my son. If he'll tell you.

[00:20] JULIAN ASHFORD (34, tailored suit, shaken) steps in from the study, the forged adoption papers in his fist. MARCUS (45, hotel manager) hovers behind him, phone in hand.

JULIAN
Mother, what did you do?

[00:30] SIENNA VALE (30, red dress, polished smile) blocks the stairs.

SIENNA
(low, to Nora)
41 minutes, Nora. Then the Department of Children and Families gets my call. Leo goes to emergency foster care tonight.

[00:40] Nora looks up. At the second-floor window, LEO (5) presses his palm to the glass.

NORA
(whispering)
I'm coming, baby.

[00:46] Julian studies Nora's face, then the child at the window. Something in him cracks.

JULIAN
How old is that boy?

[00:52] Silence. Celeste's jaw tightens. Sienna's smile dies.

NORA
(steady)
Ask your mother why you left me at the altar. Ask her what she paid my father.

[00:58] Marcus lowers the phone. Celeste goes pale.

CELESTE
(barely audible)
Nora, don't.

Julian turns to her, voice breaking.

JULIAN
Paid him... what?
