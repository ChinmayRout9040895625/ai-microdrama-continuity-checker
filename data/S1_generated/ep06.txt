EPISODE 6 - "The Signature"

[00:00] EXT. ASHFORD ESTATE GATE, BROOKLINE - NIGHT, 11:14 PM
Rain hammers the iron gate. SIENNA VALE (31, silk coat, icy calm) holds the folder out like a weapon. JULIAN ASHFORD (36, soaked tailored suit) stares at the signature page.

SIENNA
That's your handwriting, Julian. Dated March 3rd, 2019. You signed Leo over before he could even walk.

[00:12] NORA BELL (32, still in her gray Grandview housekeeping uniform) snatches the page.

NORA
He was gone in 2018. The night before our wedding. How could he sign anything in 2019?

[00:22] Julian's jaw tightens. He studies the ink.

JULIAN
The J is wrong. I never loop it. Someone practiced this.

[00:30] MARCUS (48, hotel manager, rumpled blazer) steps out of the car, phone raised.

MARCUS
Then let's ask the notary. Stamp says Harold Pruitt, Suffolk County. I can have him on the line in two minutes.

[00:40] Sienna's calm cracks for half a second. She slams the folder shut.

SIENNA
You have forty-six minutes, Nora. Two million dollars or Celeste calls Child Services and reports you for neglect. Your apartment in Dorchester. Your second job. They'll call it unfit.

[00:50] Nora's hands shake. Behind Sienna, the upstairs curtain moves. A small hand presses against the glass. LEO (6) mouths one word: "Mom."

NORA
(whispering)
Leo.

[00:56] Julian grips the gate bars. Headlights sweep the driveway. CELESTE ASHFORD (64, pearls, cold eyes) steps onto the porch holding a second folder.

CELESTE
(calling out)
Show her the real reason you disappeared, Julian.

[01:00] Julian goes pale as she lifts a faded photograph: Julian, hospital bracelet on his wrist, and Nora's late father beside him.
