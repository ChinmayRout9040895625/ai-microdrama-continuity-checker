EPISODE 11 - "Two Hours"

[00:00] INT. GRANDVIEW HOTEL - STAFF LOUNGE - NIGHT
CLOSE ON Nora Bell (32) at the window. Outside, a gray van idles under a streetlight. A man in a black cap lifts a photo of her son. Her blood runs cold.
NORA: (whispers) Leo.

[00:08] She spins. Leo (7) is gone from the couch. His red backpack lies open on the floor.
NORA: Leo! LEO!

[00:15] INT. GRANDVIEW - PENTHOUSE OFFICE - CONTINUOUS
Julian Ashford (35) stares at a clock: 11:14 PM. Ninety minutes left on the DNA test. Sienna Vale (29) watches him from the doorway, pale.
SIENNA: Julian, your mother wired $50,000 to a man named Dutch this afternoon. I saw the statement.

[00:25] Julian's phone buzzes. Nora. He answers.
NORA (V.O.): They took him, Julian. They took our son.
JULIAN: (frozen) Our...

[00:33] INT. GRANDVIEW - SERVICE CORRIDOR - NIGHT
Nora sprints past laundry carts. Marcus (48, hotel manager) blocks her, sweating.
MARCUS: Loading dock. Three minutes ago. Celeste told me to unlock the gate. I swear I didn't know why.
NORA: You knew enough.

[00:44] EXT. LOADING DOCK - NIGHT
Nora bursts out in time to see the van's taillights turn onto Atlantic Avenue. Julian skids in beside her, coat flying, keys in hand.
JULIAN: Get in. Now.

[00:52] INT. JULIAN'S CAR - MOVING
Tires scream. Julian's hands shake on the wheel. His phone lights up: CELESTE.
CELESTE (V.O.): (icy calm) Darling, stop chasing. The boy is safe, if the test results never reach you. Delete them, and he comes home.

[00:58] Julian looks at Nora. Then at the dashboard clock: 11:21 PM.
SMASH CUT TO BLACK.
