EPISODE 2 - "The Envelope"

[00:00] INT. GRANDVIEW HOTEL, PENTHOUSE SUITE - DAY
Silence. Julian Ashford (33, tailored charcoal suit, tired green eyes) stares at his mother. Nora Bell (29, gray housekeeping uniform) grips her cart handle.

JULIAN
Mother. Say it again. You paid her?

[00:10] Celeste Ashford (61, pearls, ice-cold calm) smooths her sleeve.

CELESTE
Fifty thousand dollars. She took it, Julian. She left Boston the very next week.

[00:18] NORA
(shaking)
I never touched that money.

Sienna Vale (31, designer white dress, diamond ring) steps between them.

SIENNA
Of course you'd say that. Housekeepers say a lot of things for a bigger paycheck.

[00:28] Julian ignores her. He walks to Nora, close enough to see her tremble.

JULIAN
June 9th, 2018. I waited at that church for three hours. Where were you?

NORA
Waiting for YOU. At Harbor Chapel. You never came.

[00:40] Julian turns slowly toward Celeste. Her smile twitches.

JULIAN
What did you tell her?

CELESTE
Only the truth. That you'd changed your mind.

[00:48] Marcus Doyle (45, hotel manager, nervous sweat) bursts in, tablet in hand.

MARCUS
Mr. Ashford, sorry. Security found something in housekeeping locker 114.

NORA
That's my locker.

[00:55] Marcus holds up a thick cream envelope stamped with the Ashford crest. Inside, stacks of cash. Sienna glances at Celeste. Celeste doesn't blink.

MARCUS
(whispering)
There's forty thousand dollars in here. And a note in your handwriting, Nora.

Nora's face drains of color. She has never seen it before.

SMASH CUT TO BLACK.

END OF EPISODE 2
