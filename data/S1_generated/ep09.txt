EPISODE 9 - "The Gate"

[00:00] INT. ASHFORD FOYER - NIGHT
Blue and red police lights sweep across the marble floor. Everyone freezes. SIENNA VALE (29, silk gown, pale) lowers her phone.

SIENNA
Who called them? I didn't call them.

[00:08] Julian ASHFORD (34, hotel owner, tailored suit) hasn't moved. His eyes are locked on LEO BELL (6, oversized hoodie), who hides behind NORA BELL (31, housekeeper, still in her gray Grandview uniform).

JULIAN
(hoarse)
Nora. How old is he?

[00:16] Nora's hands shake on Leo's shoulders.

NORA
He turned six in March.

Julian does the math. Seven years since the hospital. Six years and some months since the night he vanished. His face drains.

[00:26] CELESTE ASHFORD (62, pearls, ice-cold) steps between them.

CELESTE
Julian, she's using a child to get to your money. I have the receipt. Two hundred thousand dollars, paid to her father.

JULIAN
The signature is forged, Mother. Frank Bell never wrote an F like that.

[00:38] The front door opens. OFFICER DANA REYES (40, Boston PD) walks in, holding a manila envelope.

REYES
Evening. We received an anonymous tip about a document forger at this address.

Celeste's jaw tightens.

[00:46] Reyes looks at the receipt in Celeste's hand.

REYES
Ma'am, I'll need to see that paper.

Sienna backs away toward the staircase. Celeste grips the receipt tighter.

[00:52] Across the foyer, MARCUS (45, hotel manager, rumpled tie) watches from the shadows by the service door, phone in hand. He meets Nora's eyes and gives the smallest nod.

[00:57] Julian turns to Reyes, then to his mother.

JULIAN
Before you take that, Officer, I need a DNA test. Tonight.

Celeste's face goes white. Leo tugs Nora's sleeve.

LEO
Mama... why does that man look like me?
