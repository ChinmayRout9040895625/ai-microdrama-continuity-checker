EPISODE 3 - "The Frame"

[00:00] INT. GRANDVIEW HOTEL, STAFF HALLWAY - NIGHT
Marcus (50s, hotel manager) holds up an Ashford-crested envelope. Inside: stacks of cash. Nora Bell (29) stares at her open locker, frozen.

MARCUS
Forty thousand dollars. Found in locker 114. Yours.

[00:10] Julian Ashford (32, hotel owner) steps forward, jaw tight. He lifts the note.

JULIAN
"Thank you for the second payment. I'll stay away." That's your handwriting, Nora.

NORA
It looks like my handwriting. I didn't write it.

[00:22] INT. GRANDVIEW HOTEL, STAFF HALLWAY - CONTINUOUS
Sienna Vale (28, Julian's fiancee) appears at the corner, pale silk coat, a small satisfied smile she hides fast.

SIENNA
Julian, don't. Some people just take advantage.

Nora notices Sienna's fingertips: smudged with blue ink.

[00:34] NORA
Marcus, who had a key to my locker?

MARCUS
(wavering)
Housekeeping supervisor. Security. And me.

NORA
And who asked you to open it tonight?

Marcus glances at Sienna. Just for a second. Nora sees it.

[00:45] Julian sees it too.

JULIAN
Marcus. Who called you?

MARCUS
(sweating)
Ms. Ashford said there was a theft. Celeste. At 9:15.

[00:52] INT. GRANDVIEW HOTEL, STAFF ROOM - CONTINUOUS
Nora's phone buzzes. A school nurse, Boston Public Elementary. She answers, her face drains.

NORA
(whispering)
Leo's gone? What do you mean he left with a woman?

On the screen behind her, hallway security footage: a small boy, 6, holding the hand of Celeste Ashford, walking out the lobby doors.

Julian stares at the boy's face. At his own eyes looking back.

JULIAN
(barely audible)
Who is that child?

[01:00] SMASH CUT TO BLACK.
