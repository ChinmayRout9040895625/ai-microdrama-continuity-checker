EPISODE 4 - "The Boy With His Eyes"

[00:00] INT. GRANDVIEW HOTEL - SECURITY ROOM - NIGHT
A monitor freezes on Leo, 6, clutching a stuffed fox, his hand in Celeste's. JULIAN ASHFORD, 34, stares at the screen. NORA BELL, 31, in her gray housekeeper uniform, grips the desk.

NORA
That's my son. Your mother has my son.

[00:10] JULIAN
(whispering, shaken)
He has my grandfather's eyes.

Nora turns on him.

NORA
Six years ago you left me at the altar of St. Anne's with a dress and a hundred and twenty guests. Don't you dare act surprised.

[00:22] JULIAN
I never left. I got a message that you'd taken money and called it off.

He pulls out his phone. Dials. Straight to voicemail.

[00:30] INT. HOTEL LOBBY - CONTINUOUS
SIENNA VALE, 29, in a white designer coat, blocks the elevator. Blue ink stains her fingertips.

SIENNA
Julian, darling, she's lying to trap you. Everyone knows housekeepers do this.

Julian looks at her fingers. Then at Nora.

JULIAN
You wrote the note, Sienna.

Sienna's smile cracks.

[00:42] EXT. GRANDVIEW HOTEL - PARKING GARAGE LEVEL 3 - NIGHT
MARCUS, 45, hotel manager, sweating, waits beside a black Mercedes and hands Julian a key card.

MARCUS
Your mother took the boy to the Ashford estate in Brookline. Gate code changes at midnight. I can get you in, but I want immunity.

Nora snatches the key card.

NORA
You stole forty thousand dollars from me. You get nothing.

[00:52] Julian opens the passenger door for her. Nora hesitates, then climbs in.

Her phone buzzes. Unknown number. A photo: Leo asleep in a mansion bedroom. Under it, a text:
"Come alone, Nora. Or sign the papers and he never knows his father."

Beside her, Julian's phone lights up. Same photo. Caller ID: MOTHER.
