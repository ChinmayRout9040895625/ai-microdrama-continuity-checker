EPISODE 12 - "The Housekeeper's Secret"

[00:00] INT. JULIAN'S SUV - NIGHT, SOUTH BOSTON DOCKS
Rain hammers the windshield. JULIAN ASHFORD (34, hotel owner) grips the phone, Celeste's voice on speaker. NORA BELL (31, housekeeper) sits beside him, shaking.

CELESTE (V.O.)
Delete the DNA results, Julian. Tonight. Or Leo, age six, disappears for good.

[00:10] Julian looks at Nora. Years of guilt in his eyes.

JULIAN
I left you the night before our wedding because my mother threatened you. I never stopped looking for you.

NORA
Then don't lose him now.

[00:22] INT. ABANDONED WAREHOUSE, PIER 9 - CONTINUOUS
LEO BELL (6) sits on a crate clutching his toy dinosaur. DUTCH (50s, scarred, Celeste's fixer) paces, phone in hand.

[00:32] EXT. PIER 9 - NIGHT
Julian and Nora creep along the wall. SIENNA VALE (30, fiancee) steps from the shadows, a recorder in her hand. Nora freezes.

SIENNA
Celeste paid Dutch $50,000. I recorded every word of it.

JULIAN
Why help us?

SIENNA
Because I was never her daughter-in-law. I was her insurance policy.

[00:46] INT. WAREHOUSE - CONTINUOUS
Julian kicks the door open. Dutch grabs Leo, pulls a knife.

DUTCH
One step closer, Mr. Ashford.

LEO
Mommy!

Nora steps forward, hands raised, calm as a mother can be.

NORA
Leo, baby, drop to the floor. Like we practiced.

[00:54] Leo drops. Julian tackles Dutch. The knife skids across concrete. Sirens wail outside, Sienna's doing. Nora scoops Leo up.

LEO
Are you my daddy?

Julian, bleeding from his lip, nods through tears.

[00:59] EXT. PIER 9 - DAWN
Celeste's black Mercedes screeches in. She steps out, smiling, holding a folded document.

CELESTE
You forgot one thing, darling. The hotel isn't yours. It's Leo's father's... and he isn't you.

Julian's face drops.

CUT TO BLACK.
