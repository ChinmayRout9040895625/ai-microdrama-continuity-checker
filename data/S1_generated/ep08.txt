EPISODE 8 - "The Price of Silence"

[00:00] INT. ASHFORD ESTATE - FOYER - DAY
Julian (32, tailored charcoal suit) grips the hospital photo. Celeste (60, pearls, ice-calm) stands between him and Nora (29, still in her gray Grandview uniform).
JULIAN: What did you pay him, Mother?
CELESTE: Nothing that wasn't best for you.

[00:10] Sienna (30, red dress, phone in hand) taps her screen.
SIENNA: Thirty-one minutes, Nora. Then Child Services knocks on your door.
NORA: You'd use a seven-year-old as a weapon?

[00:20] Julian turns to Nora, voice cracking.
JULIAN: Seven years ago, I woke up in St. Margaret's with no memory of three weeks. Frank Bell sat by my bed. Then he was gone.
NORA: He told me you ran. He said you took a check and left me at the altar.

[00:32] INT. ASHFORD ESTATE - STUDY - CONTINUOUS
Celeste opens a safe with trembling fingers. She pulls out a bank receipt.
CELESTE: Two hundred thousand dollars. Frank signed for it himself.
NORA: My father never touched money in his life.

[00:44] Julian studies the signature, then freezes.
JULIAN: This isn't his handwriting. Frank wrote his sevens with a slash. This one doesn't have it.
Celeste's face drains.

[00:52] Through the window, Leo (7, yellow raincoat) presses his hand to the glass. Julian stares at the boy's eyes, the exact gray of his own.
JULIAN (whispering): How old is he, Nora?

[00:58] Sienna's phone buzzes. Her face goes pale as she reads.
SIENNA: Julian... the report was never filed. Someone already called the police. They're at the gate.

[01:00] SMASH CUT TO BLACK.
