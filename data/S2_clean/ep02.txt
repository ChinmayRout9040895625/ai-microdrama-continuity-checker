EPISODE 2 - "The Offer"

[00:00] INT. ST. LUKE'S HOSPITAL - DAY
SAM COLLINS (12), pale, lies hooked to monitors. Maya holds his hand. DR. PATEL (50s) stands at the foot of the bed.

DR. PATEL: The surgery has to happen within thirty days. After that, I can't promise anything.

[00:10] Maya's phone buzzes. A text from her manager: "Customer complaint. Don't come back."

MAYA: (whispering) Vanessa.

[00:16] EXT. HOSPITAL PARKING LOT - DAY
A black Rolls-Royce. Adrian Cross leans against it.

MAYA: Are you following me now?
ADRIAN: I'm making you an offer. Three months. You play my girlfriend in public. I pay for your brother's surgery. All eighty thousand.

[00:30] MAYA: Why would a man like you need a fake girlfriend?

ADRIAN: My father built Cross Capital from nothing before the crash took him five years ago. Now the board wants me married to Vanessa's family money. I want them off my back.

[00:42] Maya looks back at the hospital window where Sam sleeps.

MAYA: I go home every night to a one-room apartment on Maple Street. I don't belong in your world.

ADRIAN: You don't have to belong. You just have to pretend.

[00:52] She takes his card.

MAYA: Tomorrow. Your office. And if you lie to me, Mr. Cross, the deal is off.

[01:00] END OF EPISODE 2
