EPISODE 10 - "The Announcement"

[00:00] INT. HALE GROUP HEADQUARTERS, PRESS ROOM - DAY
Cameras flash. Richard stands at the podium with Maya at his side. Adrian waits in the front row.

RICHARD: This is my daughter, Emma Hale. And today she comes home.

[00:10] Derek strides in waving a folder.

DEREK: She's a fraud! I have an independent DNA test. Zero match. This woman is a con artist after your money.

[00:24] ADRIAN: (to Maya) Vanessa blackmailed you? Why didn't you ever tell me?

MAYA: A con artist doesn't carry her father's photograph for twenty years without knowing it.

RICHARD: This lab closed in 2019. Security, remove my nephew.

DEREK: Then ask her who really killed Grace Collins!

[00:55] Silence. Maya turns white.
