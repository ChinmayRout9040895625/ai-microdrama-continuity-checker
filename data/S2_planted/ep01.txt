EPISODE 1 - "Coffee and Contempt"

[00:00] INT. ROSIE'S DINER, CHICAGO - NIGHT
Rain hammers the windows. MAYA COLLINS (24), apron stained, balances four plates. A silver locket swings at her throat.

[00:06] ADRIAN CROSS (30), sharp suit, cold eyes, sits in a booth with VANESSA REED (28), all diamonds and disdain.

[00:10] Maya trips on Vanessa's outstretched heel. Hot coffee splashes across Adrian's jacket.

ADRIAN: Do you have any idea what this suit costs?
MAYA: More than my rent, probably. I said I'm sorry.

[00:18] VANESSA: (laughing) Adrian, don't waste your breath on diner trash.

[00:22] Maya grips the coffee pot, trembling, but holds her ground.

MAYA: This trash has worked double shifts since my mom died eight years ago. I don't need your pity, and I don't need your insults.

[00:31] Her phone buzzes. She glances at it: "ST. LUKE'S HOSPITAL."

MAYA: (into phone, low) Sam? Slow down, buddy. I know. Eighty thousand dollars. I'll find it. I promise.

[00:40] Adrian watches her. His eyes catch the locket, engraved "M.H."

ADRIAN: Who's Sam?
MAYA: My little brother. He's twelve, and his heart doesn't care how rich you are.

[00:48] Maya walks away. Adrian slides a hundred-dollar bill under his cup. Vanessa notices, and her smile hardens.

VANESSA: Since when do you tip waitresses?

[00:55] Adrian doesn't answer. He's still watching Maya through the kitchen window.

[01:00] END OF EPISODE 1
