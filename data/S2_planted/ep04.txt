EPISODE 4 - "The Locket"

[00:00] INT. HALE GROUP CHARITY GALA, BALLROOM - NIGHT

[00:24] MAYA: My mother, Grace, said I was wearing it when she found me alone at the Navy Pier carnival. I was four. I don't remember anything before that.

RICHARD: Navy Pier...

MARTIN: Mr. Hale, your speech.

RICHARD: What's your name, young lady?

ADRIAN: Is there a problem, Mr. Hale?
